Safe Installation

Installation, Inspection, and Record-Keeping

Commercial property owners have a duty of care to maintain safe conditions for employees, customers, and tenants. A poorly installed or neglected water heater can lead to carbon monoxide hazards from bad venting, scalding at the tap, bacterial growth in tanks kept too cool, and costly water damage from a failed tank. Eugene Water Heater provides commercial installations and inspections that cover venting and combustion air, gas piping sizing, relief valve and discharge line routing, expansion tanks, thermostatic mixing valves, drain pans, seismic strapping where required, and the permit and inspection process. Our written reports identify risks, assign priority levels, and recommend specific preventive actions.

The paperwork matters as much as the repair. We keep a service binder with detailed service logs, temperature readings, and relief valve test dates, giving you proof of due diligence for landlords, insurers, and inspectors. Heavy-use equipment, in kitchens, laundries, and large mechanical rooms, is put on a more frequent service schedule.

Emergency Service

After-Hours Commercial Water Heater Response

Each hour without hot water costs your business money. In a restaurant, it can bring the dish room to a halt. For a salon, it cancels appointments. In a hotel, it means refund requests, and in a multifamily building it leads to tenant disputes. Eugene Water Heater offers fast emergency water heater repair for commercial properties throughout the area, with after-hours scheduling for planned work so you don't lose a business day.

Our technicians are equipped to handle the full range of commercial water heater emergencies, including gas valve failures, burned-out heating elements, ignition and flame sensor faults, tripped high-limit switches, leaking tanks and fittings, failed recirculation pumps, and tankless error-code lockouts. We prioritize diagnosing the fault and restoring hot water as quickly as possible, valving off a failed unit so the rest of the system keeps running, then put maintenance in place to keep it from happening again. Every commercial response includes thorough documentation for your service records.

Commercial Water Heater Maintenance Programs

Preventing failures is the most cost-effective way to manage commercial water heaters. Every Eugene Water Heater program is tailored to the property's equipment, usage, and budget, and covers scheduled inspections, sediment flushing, anode rod checks, descaling for tankless units, relief valve tests, burner and vent inspection, recirculation and mixing valve checks, and temperature readings at the fixtures.
